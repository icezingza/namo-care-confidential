## 1. PURPOSE

---

## 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

**"Confidential Information"** includes:

- Technical architecture and design documents
- Firestore schema and security rules
- Financial projections and business models
- Pricing structures and packages
- Source code samples or complete codebase
- Verification results and testing scenarios
- Security audit findings
- Customer/prospect contact information
- Marketing strategy and go-to-market plans
- Any information marked "Confidential" or "Proprietary"

**Exclusions:** Confidential Information does NOT include:

- Information already in the public domain (not due to Receiving Party's breach)
- Information independently developed by Receiving Party
- Information rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligations
- Information required to be disclosed by law or court order

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

**3.1 Use Restrictions**  
Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely to:
- Evaluate potential business relationship
- Conduct due diligence
- Make business decisions regarding NaMo Care

Receiving Party shall NOT use Confidential Information to:
- Develop competing products
- Disclose to competitors
- Commercialize Confidential Information

**3.2 Protection Obligations**  
Receiving Party shall:
- Keep Confidential Information secure and confidential
- Limit access to employees with a need-to-know
- Implement reasonable security measures (password protection, encryption, etc.)
- Not disclose to third parties without Disclosing Party's consent

**3.3 Return or Destruction**  
Upon request or termination of evaluation, Receiving Party shall:
- Return all Confidential Information (hard copies, emails, files)
- Permanently delete all electronic copies
- Certify destruction in writing

Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to:

- Employees with a direct need to evaluate the opportunity (under this NDA)
- Legal counsel (under attorney-client privilege)

**Notice:** Receiving Party must inform permitted recipients of this NDA and ensure they comply with these obligations.
